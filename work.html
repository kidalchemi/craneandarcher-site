I Dream Stuff

A dream journal that helps you know yourself.

  • Catch your dreams — type or speak them the moment you wake, before they fade.
  • Read them through many lenses — mythic, psychological, astrological and more, to draw out what a dream might be saying about you.
  • Keep them as art — turn a dream into an image card, or watch it unfold as an uncanny Dream Film.
  • The Zone — a calm library of meditations, sleep stories and soundscapes for the hours around sleep.
  • Dreams that rhyme — now and then a stranger’s dream echoes your own; connect if you like.
  • Private by design — your written dreams are stored under zero-access encryption, kept as ciphertext only a key on your phone can open.
