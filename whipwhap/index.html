A game by Crane & Archer

Go in. Free them. Get them home.

Whip Whap is a gray tabby in a yellow tee with an easy life in Paradise Cove. But giants and their robots are capturing good folks all over the land, and he can't just sit back. So with his loyal bulldog Nietzsche at his side, he heads out to sneak into the villains' lairs, crack the puzzle on each cage, and bring every friend home safe.

The game · Support · Privacy

Five worlds, twenty-five levels

  • Sneak in — cross caves, mountains, castles, frozen streets and a lava disco, past robot guards.
  • Crack the cage — find the gold, blue and red stones and set them on the right pillars before time runs out.
  • The escape — lead your friends home while the robots give chase and the giant wakes up. Don't leave anyone behind!
  • Bonk, whip and whap — cartoon bonks only: robots get dizzy, then whapped away. No blood, nothing scary.
  • Play your way — Peaceful (you can't get hurt), Normal, or Nightmare for heroes only.

One price. No ads, ever.

The first level is free to play. One purchase of $3.99 opens all twenty-five levels. No ads, no loot boxes, no tracking, and every purchase sits behind a grown-up check.
